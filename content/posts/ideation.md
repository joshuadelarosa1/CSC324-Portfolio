## Ideation

![ideation snippet](/CSC324-Portfolio/images/ideation_snippet.png)

This snippet is located in our Design repository, under the file "CSC324 Needfinding and Ideation.pdf". We all worked on ideation together as a group, and Connor wrote out our process for us (thank you Connor). 

In terms of techniques, we used multiple methods of ideation. Specifically, we employed round-robin pitch ideas, reference game benchmarking and team goal alignment. After conducting our needfinding and discovering what users would like to see in a mobile game, we came up with ideas which would fufill those needs. I originally pitched a puzzle game, similar to one we had seen in class, but realized that it would be hard to incorporate Grinnell specific aspects to the game and also hard to gage difficulty level for a variety of users. Gabi and I then worked together to pitch the idea of having a Grinnell life simulator. We used 'BitLife' as our 'reference game' which would help us give the gist of what we were going for. 

When discussing each of our specific goals for the semester, I spoke about having something simple enough to be completed and have a finished product. We all agreed that we would rather start small than work our way outwards as we made progress. We ended up going with the GrinnSim idea because it fit all the requirements we had: something that Grinnellian's could relate to, a game that's challenging but not so difficult it's frustrating, and a project we could realistically complete in the given timeframe. 

This demonstrates the completion of the ideation competency because, as a group, we were able to brainstorm solutions to the user's (and our) needs and successfully identify our product as being GrinnSim.
