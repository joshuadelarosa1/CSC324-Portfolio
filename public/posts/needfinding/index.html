In order to discover users’ needs, we conducted interviews to gauge what users like and dislike about mobile games. From this process, we learned that users really value the visual looks and feel of mobile games. They also enjoy having a story aspect/motivation to continue playing the game. Balancing the difficulty of the game was also very vital in keeping users hooked on playing a mobile game. One interview, in particular, also gave us the other side of the spectrum as a user who does not really like playing mobile games. For this interviewee, the most important aspect of a mobile game was being something that is mentally stimulating, but also quick to do during the day (like NYT games).
